### What I build

Software that a business opens every morning and cannot get through the day without.
That usually means roles and permissions, money that has to reconcile, and a mobile
screen for whoever is standing away from a desk.

**[LevelUp Academy](https://levelup-academy.uz)** — multi-tenant education CRM · *architect &amp; team lead*
One backend serving many partner organisations, each scoped to its own branches, staff
and students. The hard part is money: invoices, cash and card, split payments across
several transactions, and instalment plans that must never double-charge.
`Node.js` `Express` `PostgreSQL` `Redis` `BullMQ` `Socket.io` `React` `Vite` `Tailwind` `S3`

**[Greenhouse](https://github.com/Azizbek0010/GreenHouse)** — operations &amp; anti-fraud system · *sole developer*
A flower business losing money between the greenhouse and the till. Every movement of
stock is photographed where it happens, so intake, sale and write-off reconcile later.
Cashiers split one sale across cash, card and debt; the owner reads the month against
the last one, in Tashkent time rather than the server's.
`React Native` `Expo` `Express` `MongoDB` `React` `Tailwind` `Render` `Vercel`

**[ATTO](https://github.com/Azizbek0010/ATTO-backend)** — client project · *frontend &amp; backend*
Earlier work, and where I settled how I structure an Express service.
